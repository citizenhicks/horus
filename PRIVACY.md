# möbius Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026.

möbius is a client for self-hosted AI gateways. The app developer does not
collect, sell, track, or use your personal data for advertising or analytics.

The app stores gateway addresses, preferences, and pairing credentials on your
device. Pairing credentials are stored in the Apple Keychain. When you use the
app, the content and commands you choose to send are transmitted only to the
gateway you configure. That gateway and any model provider it uses may process
the data under their own policies.

Apple may provide TestFlight or App Store diagnostics according to your device
and Apple privacy settings. You can remove locally stored möbius data by
removing gateways in the app or deleting the app.

For privacy questions, open an issue in the
[möbius repository](https://github.com/citizenhicks/mobius/issues).
